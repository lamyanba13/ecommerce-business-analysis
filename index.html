Business Context

The Story Behind The Analysis

An ecommerce business can appear successful when revenue is growing, but revenue alone does not explain whether the business is improving. Discounts can inflate sales while weakening margin, certain product categories can drive volume without delivering enough profit, and strong national performance can hide weak regional results.

This project answers that broader question: not just how much the business sold, but where value is really being created. The analysis focuses on product mix, regional variation, monthly patterns, and pricing pressure so the final recommendations feel operational rather than academic.

Analysis Flow

How The Project Moves From Raw Data To Recommendations

01

Prepare The Dataset

Convert the order date correctly, validate required fields, clean numeric columns, remove duplicates, and create reusable analysis columns such as year, month, month name, and profit margin.

02

Measure Business Performance

Calculate total revenue, total profit, average order value, and margin. Break performance down by category, product, region, and month to understand where results are strongest and where they begin to weaken.

03

Translate Findings Into Decisions

Use discount analysis, trend reporting, and segment comparisons to create recommendations around pricing strategy, promotion planning, product focus, and regional prioritization.

Recommended Actions

What The Business Should Do Next

Protect Margin During Discounting

Review deeper discount tiers carefully and avoid aggressive promotions on products already operating with weak margin.

Double Down On Strong Segments

Prioritize investment in the best-performing category and region where growth appears strongest and margin is better supported.

Use Top Products As Growth Levers

Support leading products with bundles, cross-sells, and repeat-purchase campaigns to improve basket value.
